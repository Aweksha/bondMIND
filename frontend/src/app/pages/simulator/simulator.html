What am I looking at?

The blue curve: actual price

A bond's price is the value today of all its future payments. When yields rise, those payments are worth less, so the price falls. When yields fall, the price rises.

The straight line: duration

Duration is the slope at today's yield. It says roughly how much the price moves per 1% change in yield. A duration of 6 means about a 6% price change for a 1% (100 bp) move.

The red gap: convexity

The real curve bends, so the straight line always undershoots. The red gap is that error. It grows with bigger moves. Adding convexity, the bend, closes most of the gap.
